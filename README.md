





##  How to Tell if a Side-by-Side Video is 180 or 360?

* It is VR 180 if: The images inside the panels look like two circular, fish-eye bubbles with black empty space around the edges, or if the geometric proportions look completely normal and tightly framed like a standard wide lens.
* It is VR 360 if: The images inside the panels look violently distorted, stretched out, and warped. For example, a straight line on a wall looks like a wave, or a person standing near the edge looks stretched out like a cartoon character. This is because the camera squished an entire 360-degree room into a flat rectangular frame.


## "Spherical" and "Stereoscopic" Settings:
To make an immersive video play correctly, you have to answer two different questions for the video player:

   1. The Projection Environment (Where do I look?): Is the video stretched onto a 360-degree ball, a 180-degree front dome, or left flat? (Controlled by your spherical checkboxes/dropdowns).
   2. The Stereoscopic Depth (How do I see 3D?): Is the image flat 2D, or split side-by-side/top-bottom to create a 3D effect? (Controlled by this combo_stereo dropdown).
